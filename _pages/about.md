<section class="home-section" id="about-me">
  <h2 class="home-section__title">👨‍🎓 About Me</h2>

  <p>
    I am a Ph.D. student in the Service Robotics Laboratory at the
    <a href="https://www.sdu.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Shandong University</a>,
    advised by Prof. Guohui Tian. My research focuses on service robotics,
    embodied intelligence, and long-horizon task planning.
  </p>

  <p>
    I am especially interested in building the <strong>robot brain</strong>:
    improving planning, navigation, and grasping capabilities so that robots can
    understand human instructions, reason about complex environments, and provide
    intelligent embodied assistance.
  </p>
</section>
